📋 THE PROMPT TEMPLATE \- pinned in the comments \- easier to copy from there.

💡 KEY TAKEAWAYS • Take your hardware report while the sim is running under load, not at idle • Check GPU utilisation in your worst-case scenario \- if it's under \~70%, you're CPU-bound and graphics settings won't save you • Screenshot your settings pages so the AI reads your real menus instead of recalling them • Apply changes in batches of 3-4 and test after each one • Chase consistent frame times, not a big average FPS number • Re-run this whole process after every major sim update \- settings get renamed, added or removed, and five minutes keeps you optimal instead of running last patch's settings

⚠️ A NOTE ON AI \- AI assistants make mistakes \- that's exactly why this process is built the way it is. Screenshots stop it guessing at your menus, and batch testing catches anything it gets wrong. Be especially careful with any advice touching voltages, clock speeds or fan curves, and verify BIOS changes against your motherboard manual.
